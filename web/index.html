The little secret

  1. Memorize one of the 21 cards. Don't click it.
  2. Choose the pile containing that same card.
  3. Follow your card through three rounds, then watch the reveal.

If you lose track, use Start over. Keys 1, 2, and 3 select a pile.

How does it work?

The chosen pile always goes between the other two. Dealing the cards into three piles repeatedly narrows the possible positions. After three choices, your card is the 11th card in the collected deck. No camera, tracking, or mind reading needed.
